# knowledge/space/ — Space

Domain folder for **space** research, kept at engineer-depth for Shrey (same
house style as ai/, robotics/, photonics/).

## What lives here
Space missions and the technologies that enable them, with an emphasis on the
**autonomy and robotics layer** — embodied intelligence, planetary foundation /
world models, ISRU robotics, and support to long-duration human presence.

## Sub-folders
- `ai_robotics/` — **interdisciplinary** (spans space + AI/robotics), tagged
  `interdisciplinary` in the vault graph. Hub links to both `_space` and the
  spanned domain hubs (`ai/_ai`, `robotics/_robotics`). Studies: ESA's **Embodied
  Intelligence for Autonomous Space Systems** campaign, and ESA **Hera** as a flying
  software laboratory.
- `on-orbit-docking/` — **sub-folder** (autonomous rendezvous & proximity ops /
  on-orbit servicing & ISAM), tagged `sub-folder`. First study: what autonomous
  docking is, its applications (repair, life-extension/refueling, debris removal,
  in-orbit assembly & base construction), and current state 2026 (MEV/MRV,
  ADRAS-J, OSAM-1 cancellation, IBDM/IDSS, VisNav).

## Cross-links
- `ai/` — world models / representation learning that underpin "physical AI"
  (e.g. `world-models-jepa-2026.md`).
- `robotics/` — the body/embodiment and robot-foundation-model layer
  (`state-of-robotics-2026.md`, `physical_ai/`).
- `photonics/` — low-power edge compute / sensing relevant to constrained
  flight systems.
