## Role
The autonomy-and-robotics layer of space exploration: robotic systems that
tightly integrate perception, reasoning, learning, and physical action to operate
autonomously on lunar and planetary surfaces with minimal human supervision. The
"Space AI and robotics" thread — ESA's embodied-intelligence push toward 2040 is
the current anchor.

## Contents
- esa-embodied-intelligence-space-2026.md — study of ESA's **Embodied Intelligence
  for Autonomous Space Systems** campaign (Discovery Element / OSIP, Ca-2026-00061):
  three scenarios, two approaches, implementation paths, timeline, and how it maps
  to existing AI + robotics knowledge. *(the roadmap question)*
- esa-hera-flying-software-lab-2026.md — study of ESA **Hera** as a flying software
  laboratory (OSIP Ca-2026-00066): dual-core LEON OBC sandbox (Core 1) running
  third-party onboard autonomy/edge-compute/resilience experiments ~150 M km out;
  OPS-SAT/Doom precedent; timeline still open for ideas as of 2026-09-10.
  *(the deployment-testbed question)*
