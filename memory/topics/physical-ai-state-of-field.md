## Key findings (2026-09-10)
- **Definition:** AI that senses, decides, and acts in the physical world — NVIDIA
  (Jensen Huang) framing; much bigger than humanoids → 9 verticals incl. AV,
  drones, factories, healthcare, defence, space, smart home.
- **Inflection**: 2025 = first year the full stack (perception, foundation models,
  actuation, edge compute, simulation) matured together → record >$75B capital.
- **Market (analyst)**: $430B by 2030 → ~$1T 2035–40 → ~$1.6T by 2040; three-wave
  adoption (industrial 2026–30, cross-sector 2030–40, consumer/sovereign 2035–40).
- **Value is moving**: metal → models → orchestration → safety-certification.
  "Compute is data": real-world data no longer the moat; synthetic/sim data factory
  (Cosmos+OSMO) attacks the 50–200-demo data bottleneck.
- **Binding deployment gate = safety certification** (NVIDIA Halos, BlackBerry QNX);
  constraint is "safe enough and certifiable," not "capable enough."
- **Open race**: no geography has combined intelligence (US), cost+volume (China),
  density ($65B Japan sovereign bet), physics wedge (EU Mistral/Emmi).
- **NVIDIA GTC 2026**: Cosmos 3 (unified world model), GR00T N1.7 + GR00T N2
  (DreamZero world-motion, "2× VLA" claim, top of MolmoSpaces/RoboArena), Isaac
  Lab 3.0/Newton, Physical AI Data Factory Blueprint, Jetson Thor edge standard.
- **Trackable peers**: Physical Intelligence π0.7 (VLA "GPT-3 moment" framing),
  Google Gemini Robotics, Qwen-Robot (from robotics survey).
