## Key findings (2026-09-10)
- Two industries under one word: (A) mainstream intralogistics/warehouse robotics —
  real, 52% of companies use robots, 74% meet goals, 6–12mo go-live; (B) humanoids —
  ~7–8k commercial units, mostly structured industrial pilots (Tesla 1k+ internal,
  Figure/BMW, Agility/Amazon, Mercado Libre/Agility).
- $12B+ raised by humanoid cos (2023–26), rivaling the whole industrial-robot market.
- **The shift:** value is migrating from chassis to **robot foundation models**
  (Gemini Robotics 2, Qwen-Robot, NVIDIA GR00T, Physical Intelligence, Skild AI).
  Model layer = durable moat; hardware commoditizing.
- Bottlenecks are NOT mechanical: (1) data collection cost (50–200 demos/task today,
  goal ~10–20), (2) no autonomous failure recovery, (3) safety certification.
