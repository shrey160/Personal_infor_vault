## Key findings (with dates)
- Photonics for AI is not about replacing transistors — it's an **interconnect*
  play (CPO / NVLink Fusion photonics, OCI MSA) that's deploying now, plus a
  much-early **analog photonic compute** sub-field (Q.ANT diffusion/xLSTM @ ISC
  2026; Lightelligence PACE 3 @ WAIC 2026) still fighting the ADC/DAC conversion tax.
- Comms = the most-real domain: silicon photonics + CPO mainstreaming, hollow-core
  fiber records (YOFC 1.2 Tb/s/λ; FiberHome @ IEEE SUM 2026), ~430–450 Tb/s
  single-fiber research records, OCS market >$8B/2030.
- Quantum: photonic QC split PsiQuantum (fault-tolerant, million-qubit, Global
  Foundries "Omega," NVIDIA-backed ~$7B) vs Xanadu (public, modular Aurora).
  No independent advantage demonstrated yet. Quantum *networks*/QKD earlier in
  readiness: EuroQCI IBERIANQCI live, Eagle-1 delayed, Ireland/Q*Bird QKD,
  Qunnect metro entanglement + NYC 3-node entanglement swap.
